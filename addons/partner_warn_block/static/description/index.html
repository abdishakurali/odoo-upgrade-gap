Block Customers and Products

Odoo 19 and 20 only warn about a contact or product. This module brings back the Blocking Message choice of Odoo 18: one switch, your own message, enforced on orders and invoices. Need customers with overdue invoices blocked automatically? See the paid Pro version, partner_warn_block_pro.

Why you will like it

Block sales, purchases and invoices
Tick Block next to the warning message of a contact. Each of the three is set separately.
Works for contacts of a company
A person inside a blocked company is blocked too, because the rule follows the company.
Enforced, not just shown
A blocked contact cannot be saved on a new order, and a quotation for a contact blocked later cannot be confirmed.
Products too
Block a recalled product for sales or for purchases; it cannot be added to an order line.

See it in Odoo

Screens from the module on invented demo data, shown in an Enterprise-style theme. The module runs on Odoo 19 · 20 Community and Enterprise, on Odoo.sh and on-premise (not Odoo Online).

Everything it does

Contacts

✓Block sales, Block purchases and Block invoices, each with its own message
✓Works through the company of the contact
✓The reason is shown in the warning, with a default text if you left it empty

Products

✓Block sales and Block purchases on the product form
✓Section and note lines are never blocked
✓The message tells the user which product and why

Invoices and credit notes

✓Customer and vendor invoices and credit notes are covered
✓A message for invoices is shown on the draft
✓Journal entries are never blocked

How it works

1
Install
Install the module; it needs Sales, Purchase and Invoicing.
2
Turn on the warnings
In Settings, switch on Warnings for Sales and for Purchase so the fields appear on the forms.
3
Block
On the contact, tick Block sales and write why.
4
Try it
Create an order for that contact: Odoo stops it and shows your message.

Good to know

  • For Odoo 19 and 20. On Odoo 16, 17 and 18, where Blocking Message exists but only acts in the form, a separate version of this module enforces it on the server.
  • The Block switches only appear when the standard Warnings settings of Sales and Purchase are on.
  • Blocking is enforced on orders and invoices handled in the backend; it was not tested with Point of Sale or the website shop.

Compatibility

RequiresOdoo 19.0 or 20.0 with Sales, Purchase and Invoicing (Odoo 18 has this built in).
